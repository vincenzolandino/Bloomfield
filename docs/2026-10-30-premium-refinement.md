# October 30 design refinement

This is a bounded local refinement of the existing talent-site branch. No hosting, deployment or repository push is included.

The existing typefaces and page structure remain. Headings are smaller, tracking is more relaxed, and content has a consistent maximum width. Cream and near-black carry the pages; the red inquiry sections and dark footer have been replaced with a quieter cream treatment. Red remains a small brand accent. Neutral portrait spaces retain their pending labels without oversized initials.

The homepage heading reads “Speaking, moderation and brand partnerships.” The contact heading is shorter. The repeated “Bring us the brief” line has been removed. Profile facts, public proof links, person-specific inquiry subjects, the contact inbox and anonymous agency representation remain unchanged. The roster stays on the separate Talent page.

The stylesheet and social-card URLs use a new cache version. The existing browser check now accepts BLOOMFIELD_ARTIFACT_DIR so separate review screenshots can be retained without overwriting earlier evidence.

Validation passed against the changed local site: seven Node checks; JavaScript syntax; whitespace; all five pages at seven viewport widths; 200% text at 320px and 390px; rendered text contrast; keyboard focus and skip link; no-JavaScript and reduced-motion behavior; resources; CSP and actual Netlify 200/410 route behavior. Desktop and mobile screenshots were reviewed.

The three approved portraits and final creative review remain pending. No portrait or credential was fabricated.
